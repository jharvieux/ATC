# Quickstart — Subscription (Sub-host) tiers

> **Available on:** Starter, Pro, Agency subscription tiers.

Welcome. This page walks you through your first hour if you signed up
on a subscription tier — meaning you pay a monthly fee and the platform
handles the AI cost for you.

There is no jargon here. If a word doesn't make sense, search the help
center — and if it still doesn't, click **Report a question** so we can
explain it better.

## Step 1 — Confirm your subscription is active

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. You should see **Status: Active** in green.

[Screenshot: Settings → Billing with the Active status badge]

If it says **Past due** or **Incomplete**, click the link to update
your payment method. The concierge keeps working through one missed
payment, but two in a row pauses your tenant.

## Step 2 — Set your branding (5 minutes)

You can launch without branding, but customers respond better when they
see your name and colors.

1. Open **Settings → Branding**.
2. Paste logo URLs (light mode and dark mode — they can be the same).
3. Pick your primary, secondary, and accent colors.
4. Write a one-line slogan.
5. Click **Save**.

See the [Branding](./03-branding) help page for the full list of fields.

> **About the "Powered by AI Travel Concierge" message:** Starter tier
> includes this message on your concierge. Pro and Agency hide it.
> See [Branding](./03-branding) for details.

## Step 3 — Invite teammates

1. Open **Settings → Team and permissions**.
2. Click **Invite a teammate**, enter their email, pick a role.

> **Team size limits by tier:**
>
> - Starter — 1 user
> - Pro — up to 5 users
> - Agency — up to 25 users
>
> Need more? Email support to discuss an Enterprise plan.

See [Team and permissions](./09-team-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## Step 4 — Customize a persona (Pro and above)

Your concierge has six built-in AI personalities (called "personas").
Each one has a different specialty — luxury cruises, family travel,
group bookings, and so on.

On Pro and Agency tiers, you can give a persona extra instructions
("always recommend our preferred suppliers", "never quote pricing
without checking the live calendar").

1. Open **Settings → AI Personas**.
2. Click any persona to expand.
3. Scroll to **Persona addendum** and type your instructions.
4. Click **Submit for screening**.

[Screenshot: Settings → AI Personas with an expanded persona card showing the addendum editor]

The platform's safety check reviews your addendum within a minute. If
it's approved, the AI uses your instructions immediately. If it's
rejected, the page shows the reason and you can edit and re-submit.

See [Personas](./04-personas) for ideas of what to put in the addendum.

## Step 5 — Share your concierge with one customer

Don't tell everyone yet. Pick one customer you trust:

1. From your home dashboard, copy the link under **Your concierge URL**.
2. Send it to one customer with a note: "Try our new AI assistant — let
   me know what works and what doesn't."
3. Watch the **Conversations** page to see what they ask.

This soft launch with one customer catches 90% of surprises before you
share with your whole list.

## What to do next

- **First week:** check **Conversations** daily for issues. Use
  **Mark as bad answer** when the AI says something wrong — that
  teaches the platform.
- **First month:** read [Usage and billing](./08-usage-and-billing) so
  you know what your monthly bill includes.
- **When ready to scale:** see
  [RAG content](./07-rag-content) for how to feed your concierge your
  own knowledge — your travel guides, supplier lists, and FAQs.
