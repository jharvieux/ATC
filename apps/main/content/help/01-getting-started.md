Welcome to the AI Travel Concierge platform admin console. This guide walks
you through the first 30 minutes after you complete signup.

## Activation checklist

After signup, you'll see an onboarding checklist on the home dashboard.
Complete each item in order:

1. **Confirm business identity** (legal name, support contact, mailing address)
2. **Pick a tier** — Research, Professional, or Agency
3. **Connect Stripe** for billing
4. **Set up branding** (optional but recommended before launch)
5. **Add team members**
6. **Submit for review** when ready

The review is performed by the platform compliance team and typically
completes within 1 business day.

## What happens after activation

Once your tenant is **active**, your AI travel concierge is live for any
customer who lands on your subdomain. You can monitor activity in the
admin dashboard, and any customer feedback or escalations flow through
the chat surface.

## Next steps
