# Quotes and bookings

> **Available on:** all tiers.

The Quotes section is where conversations turn into business. When the
AI (or your team) is ready to send a customer a formal price, it
creates a quote. When the customer says yes, the quote becomes a
booking.

Open **CRM → Quotes**.

[Screenshot: CRM Quotes list view showing several quotes with statuses]

## Quote statuses

- **Draft** — being put together; not yet sent to the customer.
- **Sent** — emailed to the customer, awaiting their reply.
- **Accepted** — customer said yes. Becomes a booking automatically.
- **Declined** — customer said no; the platform keeps it for reference.
- **Expired** — quoted price is past its validity date.

## How a quote gets created

Three common paths:

### 1. The AI drafts it during a chat

When a customer says "Yes, please send me a quote for that
itinerary," the AI assembles a draft quote with:

- Cruise line, ship, sail date, itinerary
- Cabin category and price
- Add-ons (insurance, excursions, drink package)
- Terms and conditions

In **Draft only** AI mode, the quote arrives in your quotes list as a
Draft for you to review and send. In **Autonomous** mode, the AI sends
it directly to the customer.

### 2. You create it by hand

For phone-call follow-ups or complex itineraries:

1. **CRM → Quotes → New quote**.
2. Pick the contact.
3. Fill in the cruise details, cabin, price, validity window.
4. Click **Save and send** (or **Save as draft** to send later).

[Screenshot: Quote creation form with line items]

### 3. You convert a conversation

In a customer chat, click the **Create quote** button in the top right.
The form opens pre-filled from the chat context.

## Sending the quote

When you click **Send**, the platform:

1. Generates a PDF using your branding.
2. Emails it to the customer with a "View and accept" link.
3. Logs the send time and the email-delivery confirmation.

The customer's accept link opens a one-page summary with a green
**Accept this quote** button. When they click it, the quote auto-
converts to a booking.

## Bookings

A booking is a confirmed reservation. The booking page shows:

- All the quote details
- The booking confirmation number from the cruise line
- Payment schedule (deposit, second deposit, final payment)
- Documents (e-tickets, luggage tags, insurance certificate)

[Screenshot: Booking detail page with the payment schedule and documents panel]

### Recording payment

When the customer pays a deposit, log it:

1. Open the booking.
2. Click **Record payment**.
3. Pick the payment line item, enter the amount and method.
4. Click **Save**.

The booking's outstanding balance updates automatically, and the
customer gets a payment-received email.

### Cancellations

If the customer cancels:

1. Open the booking.
2. Click **Cancel booking**.
3. Enter the cancellation reason and any refund amount.

The platform records this against the original quote and updates your
reports.

## Group bookings (Pro and above)

> **Available on:** Pro, Agency, BYO Agency.

For group cruises (10+ cabins), use **CRM → Quotes → New group quote**.
Group quotes have shared pricing, a group leader contact, and bulk
cabin assignment. See the Group Tools section for the full workflow.

## Reports

**CRM → Reports** shows:

- Quotes sent / accepted / declined per month
- Average quote value
- Win rate by persona
- Lead source breakdown

Use these to spot trends — for example, a low win rate on quotes from
a specific persona might mean the persona's tone isn't matching that
customer segment.

## Frequently asked

**Can a customer counter-offer?** Yes. When they reply to the quote
email, the message lands in the conversation as a new message; you or
the AI can revise the quote and resend.

**The cruise line price changed before the customer accepted.** Open
the quote, click **Update prices**, and the platform offers to send a
revised version to the customer.

**A booking shows the wrong cabin.** Open the booking, click **Edit
booking**, fix the cabin field, save. The cruise line confirmation is
separate — call the cruise line if their system needs updating too.
