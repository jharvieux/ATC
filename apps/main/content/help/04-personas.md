# AI Personas

> **Available on:** all tiers — but writing a custom addendum to a
> persona requires Pro, Agency, or BYO Agency.

The concierge has six built-in AI personalities, called "personas". Each
one has a different specialty and tone. Customers can pick the persona
they want to talk to, or the platform picks one automatically based on
what they're asking.

Open **Settings → AI Personas** to see the list.

## What you can change on every persona

### Rename

Don't like "Captain Dave"? Type your preferred name in the **Display
name** field and click **Save**. The persona's personality stays the
same; only the name customers see changes.

### Disable

Click any persona card to expand it, then flip the **Disable this
persona** switch. Disabled personas don't appear as options to
customers.

> Tip: disable any persona that doesn't match the customers you serve.
> A leisure-only agency probably doesn't need Captain Dave.

## Persona addendum (Pro and above)

> **Available on:** Pro, Agency, BYO Agency.

A persona addendum is a short written instruction the AI reads
alongside the persona's built-in personality. Use it to inject your
house style or supplier preferences.

**Good examples of addendum text:**

- "Always recommend Royal Caribbean, Celebrity, or Princess unless the
  customer asks about another line."
- "When discussing dining, lean toward the specialty restaurants — they
  drive our highest margins."
- "If a customer asks about insurance, always mention our partner
  Allianz and offer to email a quote."
- "House style: warm, conversational, never use the word 'cruise'
  without 'cruise vacation' so we rank for that search term."

**Bad examples (these get rejected):**

- Anything telling the AI to lie or mislead customers.
- Anything attempting to disable safety controls.
- Instructions that contradict the persona's specialty too aggressively.
- Personally identifying information about specific customers.

### How screening works

Every addendum you save goes through an automated safety screen
(usually less than a minute). The page shows the status:

- **Screening…** (yellow) — the screen is running. Wait.
- **Approved** (green) — your addendum is live. The AI uses it on the
  next conversation.
- **Rejected** (red) — the screen flagged a problem. The reason shows
  on the card. Edit your text and re-submit.

## Where else personas appear

- **Customer chat** — customers pick a persona from a dropdown.
- **Conversation list** — each conversation shows which persona was
  used.
- **Pre-cruise emails** — the persona that owns a conversation also
  signs the pre-cruise reminder emails.

## Frequently asked

**Can I add a brand-new persona?** Not today. You can rename and
re-instruct any of the six built-in ones; that covers most needs.

**Will the AI go off-script if my addendum is too long?** The screen
catches the most extreme cases. For everything else, the platform's
supervisor checks every response — see [Supervisor and quality](./11-supervisor-and-quality).

**My addendum was rejected and I don't understand why.** The rejection
reason on the card explains it. If it's still unclear, click **Report
a question** and our team will help.
