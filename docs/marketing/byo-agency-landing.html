Questions

Things agents ask us first

Do I have to leave my current host agency?

No — that's the whole point. You bring your own host-agency credentials and keep booking through Travel Leaders, Avoya, Cruise Planners, or whoever you use today. Your commission relationship doesn't change. We're the AI and software layer on top, not a new host.

Where do my commissions go?

Straight to your existing host agency, exactly as they do now. AI Travel Concierge never sits between you and your commission — you pay us a flat subscription for the software, and that's it.

How does the free trial work?

Every paid tier includes a 30-day free trial. The clock starts when you activate your workspace — not the moment you sign up — and you're not charged during the trial. Cancel any time before it ends and you won't pay a thing.

Can I put my own brand on it?

Yes, on the Agency tier. You get a custom domain, send from your own email-from address, can remove the "Powered by" branding, and can rename and fine-tune the AI personas to match your agency's voice. Professional lets you rename the personas; Agency lets you fine-tune how they speak.

What if I'm a one-person shop?

Start on Research ($19/mo) to try the AI chat, CRM, and quoting, or go straight to Professional ($59/mo) for the full single-agent toolkit including Gmail and the supervisor dashboard. You can upgrade to Agency and add seats whenever you grow.

Is the AI safe to put in front of my clients?

Yes. Every AI reply runs through an automated supervisor that checks for accuracy, tone, over-promising, pricing math, and compliance language before it's sent, and escalates to you when a human should take over. Clients always see a clear "AI-assisted chat" disclosure.
