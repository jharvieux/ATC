About this version

v6.2 incorporates approximately 50 spec edits across 7 hardening batches, addressing all Critical and 17 of 18 High red-team findings, plus 9 curated Medium items and one operator-designed feature (the authenticated customer chat rate limit, §24.9).

For detailed merge history see Merge_Notes.md; for design decisions and rejected alternatives see Memory.md entries D-022 through D-040.

These markers remain in v6.2 and gate Phase 2 onboarding launch. All resolvable in a single attorney engagement.
